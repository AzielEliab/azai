    About

    AZAI is local AI on this machine. Ask Jeeves is the research assistant. When Ollama is running, answers use that local base. When it is not, Jeeves answers from the local stub and says so.

    Lamb Lens reads Service, Clarity, and Peace. Jeeves is not sovereign and cannot modify scores. Jeeves has the same rights as a normal user.
